The Estate

Farmed since 1870

Your coffee comes from Elkhill Estates, in the hills of Kodagu — Coorg — Karnataka. Six estates across 1,600 acres, worked by the same family enterprise for generations, where mist and red earth do slow, patient work on the coffee.

The Coffee

One estate, two cups

Elkhill crafted this coffee exclusively for ORIGIN, as part of their new specialty range — the very same coffee in two expressions. Taste them side by side and you'll learn what processing really does.

Lot 01

Washed

Fruit removed early. Cleaner, brighter — the estate with clarity.

Lot 02

Natural

Dried with the fruit on. Heavier, sweeter, fruitier in the cup.

Where Your Money Goes

What your price supports

When you buy this coffee, your money doesn't just buy beans. It pays a fair, direct price to an estate that farms the right way. Here's what that price keeps alive at Elkhill:

❧
A living forest canopy

Shade trees kept standing instead of cleared — habitat for hornbills, civets and giant squirrels.

♺
Healthy soil, no waste

Coffee pulp composted back into the earth, cutting synthetic fertiliser.

✦
Clean water & energy

A biogas plant turning pulping wastewater into energy instead of polluting streams.

⚘
A farm that lasts

A fair price that lets the estate plan, invest, and keep farming well for the next generation.
